Pixel Beacon: “رسالة إلى الفضاء” storyboard

12.4 seconds, Arabic, for kids. Everything reads right to left, so motion and arrows flow from right to left. These are still frames drawn with the real animation code.

Facts and choices I assumed

  • The drawing is the game’s own 11 × 13 example (143 squares = 143 bits), the same one the booth loads with the Example button.
  • The signal uses the game’s tones: high (1,320 Hz) for 1, low (880 Hz) for 0. In the video the 143 bits play in about 1.8 seconds.
  • Arecibo: the real message was sent from the Arecibo telescope in Puerto Rico in 1974. It was 1,679 bits, which is 23 × 73.
  • Digits are shown as 1 and 0, matching what the game shows on screen.
  • Arabic text uses the rounded Baloo Bhaijaan 2 font, because Fredoka and Nunito have no Arabic letters. Nunito is still used for the digits.
  • The QR pattern is decorative, not a real code.
